Frequently asked questions

Is this a book or a workbook?

It is a guided self-discovery workbook. It includes explanations, but its main value comes from the exercises, worksheets, decision tools and planning pages you complete.

Who is this designed for?

It is designed for adults who feel uncertain about their direction, want to understand their values and strengths better, or want a practical structure for moving from reflection to action.

How long does it take to complete?

You can work through the discovery sections in several focused sessions. The execution portion is designed to support your next 90 days through practical planning and review.

What format will I receive?

You receive a digital PDF that can be read on a phone, tablet or computer, or printed for handwritten use.

Can I work through it at my own pace?

Yes. There is no requirement to finish the workbook in one sitting. Move through the exercises at a pace that allows you to think carefully about your answers.

Is this therapy or professional advice?

No. Know Yourself is an educational personal-development resource. It does not replace professional mental-health care, financial advice or career counseling.
